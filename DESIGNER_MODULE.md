# Designer module — review draft

`/designer-studio` is a separate shirt-and-trouser recommender named **Designer** in the site navigation. The module does not generate new fabrics or claim that a catalogue swatch is physically in stock. Fabric choice, occasion, climate, desired expression, shirt collar/fit, tucked or untucked wear, self-fabric or white contrast collar/cuffs, trouser cut/rise and additional tailoring details can vary; a selected combination is assessed without inventing a garment SKU. The page uses a light editorial design, two public-domain Met Museum archive images credited under CC0, and LLinen Earth's own catalogue swatches for actual selections. A photographic mannequin composition updates on fabric selection with no per-look AI image call. It samples the cloth field of each swatch and retains the mannequin's photograph lighting; the tucked option uses a separately photographed tucked shirt, belt loops and trousers, and the white details are colour previews. Only two untucked trouser silhouettes and one tucked straight-leg construction have photo templates so far; other chosen details are clearly marked as not shown. [DESIGNER_VISION.md](DESIGNER_VISION.md) records the end goal, the photo template coverage, current formulas, research provenance and required cloth facts.

The route is a review draft until the working rules, safe fallback list, physical textile details, photo template coverage and customer-facing tone are approved. It has no suit/blazer reasoning or ranked multi-fabric options yet. The mannequin is a photo-based visual composition, not a verified simulation of the chosen cloth or individual fit. Better-tier rankings across physically verified outfits and feedback-based tuning require real customer use under the supplied build sequence and are deliberately deferred.

## Local verification

`npm run build && npm run quality` checks the page and deterministic scenarios, including cut incompatibilities, absent or confirmed weight/season/climate, MF accent rules when verified data is supplied, a formal print, two prominent patterns, misleading colour-name matches, alternate-cut eligibility and material evidence coverage.
